# Finite Forge with a possible two-to-three-year finish

The user explicitly requires no endless Forge upgrades and possible full
completion after roughly2–3years. Labs should help overcome later walls by
strengthening combat/economy. Original wording and continuation:
[FORGE_YEARS_001](../tasks/FORGE_YEARS_001.md).

This supersedes the earlier endless Tempering decision only for Forge. It does
not remove continuing Rift play, change Lab work/caps, reset saves, alter
Masterwork record bands, or authorize unrelated balance changes.

Implementation choices within the requested scope:

- Battle375, Arcane325, Harvest315; additive +1percentage-point effects retained.
- Keep all first300 Tempering prices. After that boundary marginal price growth
  is0.2% rather than10%, making the final ranks finite and attainable.
- Masterwork remains500 with its existing five-level record bands. Keep its
  first50 prices; after that boundary marginal price growth is2.5% rather than35%.
  The original500-rank price was about4.20e71Lumen, incompatible with the requested
  finite goal at measured current farm incomes. No retroactive spending/refund.
- All other caps/prices/effects stay. Paid above-cap levels retain their full
  previously earned effects, ownership, queues and Deed counts. No schema change.
- Existing damage/critical and resource Labs strengthen capped Forge combat and
  farming; no new hard calendar wait or automatic Lab-level grant in the game.

Calibration is a model, not a guaranteed calendar date: a mature fixed-Rift1001
benchmark, earning and reinvesting with real production prices, finishes all
active Forge paths in 994 productive days. Lesser-research/lower-farm and changing
income comparisons remain slower. The model grants record10000/Wisps10000,
legacy economy250 and completed Labs up front; it extrapolates actual60-second
combat samples to24productive hours/day. It does not simulate a fresh account's
research funding, unlock reachability, Empowers or changing human choices.
See [evidence](../qa/forge-years-001/README.md). Required CI, Android delivery
and unobserved human/device acceptance remain governed by the feature checkpoint.

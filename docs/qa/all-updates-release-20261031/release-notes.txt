- Tireless Vigil now requires 100 Ascends, Rift 250 and 10,000 manual taps. The Forge Never Sleeps requires 200 paid Forge levels, Rift 350 and 100 completed Lab levels across ten subjects. Previously earned rewards and automation stay unlocked.
- Navigate Rifts directly with previous, next and back-ten buttons. The farming picker has been removed, and forward navigation can resume Push.
- Guardian Tap starts at 20 damage plus the existing 2% of party power, making opening taps more useful while Wisps lead ongoing progression.
- Auto-Ascend Ritual now requires 25 Ascensions, Rift 150 and 50 completed Lab levels across six subjects, alongside its existing 100-Comet price. Existing owners retain it.
- Lab Speed Up has 8/12/24-hour duration buttons, eight speed presets, a clear Mote quote and editable next settings. Alternate slider controls remain available.
- Timing fixes preserve offline/online consistency, Mote deadlines and research-only returns.

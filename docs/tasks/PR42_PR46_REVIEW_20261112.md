## Decision and evidence

Keep PR42 open. The three continuing Forge tracks use additive effects and
exponentially growing prices while bounded utility caps remain. The eight Lab
first-start gates implement the player's chosen Rift-plus-research requirements;
completed levels and active/paused paid work retain access. The exact-head
focused suite passed 528 assertions and all four defect controls again, including
real purchases, save/recovery, access boundaries and live/offline queue parity.
Its eight applicable historical CI workflows passed. No evidence found in this
review justifies discarding this proposal. Merge conflicts, current-main
integration, Android checks and human pacing acceptance still need work.

Close PR46 in its present form. The latest product direction is mostly idle,
with brief daily check-ins. The proposed automatic-only wait creates a strong
incentive for repeated manual actions while leaving rapid manual reward farming
intact. This is a design judgement supported by the following controlled
comparison, not a claim to have measured boredom or player retention.

| Two-minute synthetic Rift29 policy | Released main Ascends / Prisms | PR46 Ascends / Prisms |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Automatic, live | 242 / 15,004 | 3 / 244 |
| Automatic, offline | 242 / 15,004 | 3 / 244 |
| Manual, at most once per second after target | 121 / 7,742 | 121 / 7,742 |

All policies use the same mature initial fixture from PR46's focused suite.
One reset is seeded ready at time zero. Subsequent combat is production
simulation; no depth/health/wallet edits occur after seeding, and no upgrades
are reinvested. Manual calls execute the production Ascend owner and its save
transaction; rewards survive reload. This is not a UI gesture or player-save
playthrough. PR46 therefore changes the tested manual/automatic Prism ratio
from about 0.52 to 31.73 without removing the manual farming route.

PR46's own 67 focused checks, including the negative control, pass. Its existing
Lab long-tail core also passed 353 checks/four controls on the candidate source
when rerun locally. Historical CI remains failed: pre-merge stopped in the
multi-command Lab long-tail step; Shared UI's standalone Rift cosmetics command
failed while the visual-profile steps passed. Logs alone do not identify the
precise underlying assertions. These failures and conflicts were not the basis
for closing the proposal, and this review does not claim to repair them.
